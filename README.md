## English

### One AI workspace for photos, images, videos, and creative effects

**AIEnhancer** is a browser-based AI enhancer and visual creation platform for creators, marketers, designers, memory keepers, and small creative teams. It brings AI photo enhancement, AI image generation and editing, AI video creation, focused effects, model selection, and asset management into one connected workspace.

The platform is designed to make modern generative media easier to use. Instead of learning a different interface for every model, users begin with the result they want, provide a prompt or reference, choose compatible settings, review the Credit estimate, and generate. Useful outputs remain available in the shared asset library for comparison, download, and future iteration.

[Start creating](https://aienhancer.co/create) · [Explore models](https://aienhancer.co/models) · [Browse AI Effects](https://aienhancer.co/effects)

### Connected image workflows

#### Text to image

Begin with a written idea and turn it into a polished image. Describe the subject, composition, environment, lighting, style, and intended use, then select the model and settings that fit the result. Text-to-image creation is useful for campaign concepts, social graphics, posters, product scenes, thumbnails, storyboards, and visual exploration.

#### Image to image

Use an existing image as the visual starting point. Explore new styles, treatments, environments, compositions, or campaign variations while keeping the subject and details that matter. This workflow gives the AI image enhancer a clear reference and makes side-by-side creative iteration easier.

#### Photo restoration and transformation

Focused tools such as old photo restoration, background changes, portrait treatments, animation, and family-photo Effects make common transformations easier to repeat. A clear source image, realistic expectations, and one controlled change at a time usually produce the most useful results.

### Connected video workflows

#### Text to video

Turn a scene description into motion. A useful video prompt normally defines the subject, action, location, camera behavior, atmosphere, and finish. AIEnhancer validates the selected settings and routes the task to a compatible production-tested model.

#### Image to video

Upload a still image, describe the intended movement, and create a video that uses the source image as its visual anchor. The workflow can support product motion, character animation, cinematic camera movement, atmospheric scenes, social content, and early visual concepts.

#### Reference and frame-guided video

Reference-to-video and frame-to-video workflows provide additional control when a project should follow existing visual material. Depending on the chosen workflow and model, references can guide identity, style, composition, continuity, or the direction between key frames.

### A practical creation workflow

1. **Choose the outcome** — Start with an image, video, or focused Effect rather than a provider name.
2. **Select the input mode** — Use a text prompt, source image, reference material, or frames supported by the workflow.
3. **Describe the result** — Define the subject, action, environment, composition, camera, lighting, style, and intended channel as clearly as needed.
4. **Choose compatible settings** — Review available models, aspect ratios, resolutions, durations, and model-specific controls.
5. **Check the Credit estimate** — Understand the expected cost before submitting the task.
6. **Generate and monitor** — Follow the task while AIEnhancer processes the selected route.
7. **Review and compare** — Inspect the output beside earlier attempts and change one meaningful variable for the next iteration.
8. **Save and reuse** — Keep successful results in Assets so a useful direction can support future versions.

### Designed for useful iteration

The first generation is often a starting point rather than the final asset. AIEnhancer supports a more deliberate iteration loop:

- preserve strong outputs instead of replacing them;
- compare prompt, model, ratio, resolution, and reference changes;
- revise one creative variable at a time when diagnosing a result;
- reuse a successful image direction as the foundation for motion;
- keep related image, video, and Effect outputs in one library;
- return reserved Credits to their original balance when a generation job fails, according to the current platform workflow.

Actual output depends on prompt clarity, source-media quality, selected model, supported controls, account limits, Credit availability, and platform content rules.

### Prompting principles

For images, describe the **subject**, **composition**, **environment**, **lighting**, **style**, and **finish**. For video, add the **action**, **camera movement**, **timing**, and **atmosphere**. When using a reference, state what should remain consistent and what may change.

A practical prompt is specific enough to guide the model but not overloaded with conflicting instructions. If a result misses the goal, keep the strongest parts and change one important detail in the next attempt.

### Responsible creation

Users should upload only material they own or are authorized to use. Before publishing or using generated media commercially, review source rights, likeness and privacy considerations, the selected plan, applicable model-provider terms, and the rules of the destination platform. Generated content should be reviewed by a person whenever accuracy, brand safety, or real-world representation matters.

### 相互连接的图片工作流

#### 文字生成图片

从一个文字创意开始，将其转化为精致图片。描述主体、构图、环境、光线、风格和目标用途，再选择适合结果的模型与参数。文字生成图片可用于营销概念、社交配图、海报、产品场景、缩略图、分镜和视觉探索。

#### 图片生成图片

使用已有图片作为视觉起点。在保留重要主体与细节的同时，探索新的风格、处理方式、环境、构图或营销变体。这个流程为 AI 图片增强提供明确参考，也让并排比较不同创意方向变得更容易。

#### 照片修复与转换

老照片修复、背景更换、人像处理、照片动画和家庭影像特效等专用工具，让常见转换流程更容易重复使用。清晰的源图片、合理预期，以及每轮只修改一个关键因素，通常更有助于获得实用结果。

### 相互连接的视频工作流

#### 文字生成视频

将场景描述转化为动态画面。实用的视频提示词通常包括主体、动作、地点、镜头行为、氛围和成片质感。AIEnhancer 会校验所选参数，并把任务交给兼容且经过生产验证的模型。

#### 图片生成视频

上传静态图片、描述希望出现的运动，并使用源图片作为视觉锚点创建视频。该流程可用于产品动态展示、角色动画、电影感镜头运动、氛围场景、社交内容和早期视觉概念。

#### 参考素材与关键帧引导视频

当项目需要遵循已有视觉素材时，参考素材生成视频和关键帧生成视频可以提供更多控制。根据所选工作流与模型，参考内容可以引导角色身份、风格、构图、连续性或关键帧之间的发展方向。

### 实用创作流程

1. **选择目标结果** — 从图片、视频或专用特效开始，而不是先选择供应商名称。
2. **确定输入模式** — 使用工作流支持的文字提示、源图片、参考素材或关键帧。
3. **描述成品方向** — 根据需要清晰定义主体、动作、环境、构图、镜头、光线、风格和目标渠道。
4. **选择兼容参数** — 查看当前可用模型、宽高比、分辨率、时长和模型专属控制项。
5. **确认积分预估** — 提交任务前了解预计消耗。
6. **生成并跟踪任务** — 在 AIEnhancer 处理所选路线时查看任务状态。
7. **查看与比较结果** — 将输出与早期尝试进行比较，并在下一轮只调整一个有意义的变量。
8. **保存并复用** — 将有效结果保存在素材库中，为后续版本继续提供支持。

### 提示词原则

创建图片时，可以描述**主体、构图、环境、光线、风格和成片质感**。创建视频时，可进一步加入**动作、镜头运动、节奏和氛围**。使用参考素材时，需要说明哪些内容应保持一致，哪些部分可以变化。

实用提示词应当足够明确，但不要堆叠相互冲突的要求。如果结果偏离目标，请保留表现最好的部分，并在下一轮只改变一个重要细节。

### 负责任地创作
